The third edition of our tutorial on uncertainty quantification for large language models, presented at ECIR-2026. Alongside hallucination detection, this edition covered applications to retrieval-augmented generation, test-time compute scaling for reasoning, and AI agents.

Presenters and authors: Artem Shelmanov, Maxim Panov, Ekaterina Fadeeva, Artem Vazhentsev, Roman Vashurin, Lyudmila Rvanova, and Timothy Baldwin.
