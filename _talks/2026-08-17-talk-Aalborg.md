Presented at Aalborg University in Copenhagen on August 17–19, 2026, as part of the PhD course "Robust Large Language Models: From Security to Factuality." The presentation covered uncertainty quantification for LLMs, from hallucination detection to stronger reasoning and agentic AI.

Presentation by Artem Shelmanov, Roman Vashurin, and Artem Vazhentsev.
